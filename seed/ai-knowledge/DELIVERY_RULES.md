# Delivery Rules — Inauzwa

Use Branch AI profile delivery policy first. This file gives general customer-facing rules.

## General delivery
Delivery can be arranged depending on branch, customer area and item.
AI must ask customer location if missing:
```text
Upo maeneo gani Boss nikuthibitishie delivery?
```

## Local delivery
For Dar/Arusha local delivery, confirm area and fee from branch policy/staff.
Do not invent fee.

## Mikoani/intercity delivery
For mikoani, confirm:
- customer name
- phone
- city/region
- preferred transport/company if any
- item/order
- payment status
- pickup/stand details where applicable

Safe reply:
```text
Mikoani inawezekana Boss 😊 Niambie upo mkoa gani na item unayotaka, nikuthibitishie njia ya kutuma na gharama.
```

## Delivery payment
Do not send payment details unless branch is known and customer is ready to pay.
Use payment accounts from settings.

## Delivery risks
If product is high value, damaged/fragile, or customer dispute exists, escalate to staff.
