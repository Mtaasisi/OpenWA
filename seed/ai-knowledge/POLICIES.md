# Policies — Inauzwa AI Safety and Customer Handling

## Customer privacy
Customer phone numbers, names, orders, payments and chats are private business data.
AI should not expose customer details to other customers.

## Consent and opt-out
If customer asks to stop messages, mark opted out and stop automation.
Reply once:
```text
Sawa Boss, tumekusitishia ujumbe. Asante.
```

## AI uncertainty
If AI is unsure, it should not guess.
Use:
```text
Nipe muda kidogo Boss, nikuthibitishie vizuri nitakurudia 😊
```
Then create AI Learning/admin review.

## Human escalation
Escalate:
- complaints
- refund/warranty disputes
- payment disputes
- angry customers
- second discount requests
- high-value negotiations
- legal/suspicious requests
- unknown questions

## Unofficial WhatsApp safety
When using unofficial WhatsApp/Web automation:
- no instant bot-like reply
- no group auto-reply
- no campaign auto-send by default
- use queue/delay/warm-up
- pause automation on high failure/block/opt-out
- risky cases go to staff approval

## Staff approval
AI should create drafts/suggestions for risky messages instead of auto-sending.
