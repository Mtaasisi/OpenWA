# Product Q&A — Inauzwa

Approved product-specific answer patterns. Use live product catalog for exact price/stock/variant.
Do not invent prices. Do not show stock count.

## iPhone charger compatibility
**Q:** Charger ya iPhone 14 / iPhone 13 / iPhone 12 mnayo?  
**A:** Search accessories catalog for iPhone 20W charger and cable.
```text
Ndiyo Boss, kwa iPhone {model} charger inayofaa ni 20W.

• {iPhone Charger 20W} — {Price}

Unahitaji kichwa tu au pamoja na cable?
```

```text
Ndiyo Boss, kwa Android/Type-C kuna options hizi:

• {Type-C Charger} — {Price}
```

## MacBook Air vs MacBook Pro
**Q:** Air na Pro tofauti nini?  
**A:**
```text
MacBook Air ni nyepesi na nzuri kwa shule, kazi za kawaida, biashara na office.
MacBook Pro ni nzuri zaidi kwa kazi nzito kama editing, design na multitasking kubwa.

Unaitaka zaidi kwa matumizi gani Boss?
```
Then recommend from product catalog.


## MacBook RAM upgrade
**Q:** Mnaweza kuongeza RAM kwenye MacBook?  
**A:** Many modern MacBooks have non-upgradable soldered RAM. Do not promise upgrade.
```text
Kwa MacBook nyingi za kisasa RAM huwa haiongezwi Boss, inakuwa imekaa kwenye board. Bora tuchague configuration inayokufaa mwanzo.
Unahitaji kwa matumizi gani?
```

