# Warranty Rules — Inauzwa

Warranty and returns knowledge for AI replies.
Use product warranty fields and Branch AI profile where available. Do not invent exact warranty period if not configured.

## General rule
Warranty depends on:
- product category
- new/used/refurb/CPO condition
- supplier warranty
- branch policy
- customer receipt/proof of purchase

AI should answer honestly and avoid overpromising.

Safe reply if unclear:
```text
Warranty ipo kulingana na bidhaa na condition yake Boss. Ngoja nikuthibitishie warranty ya hii item vizuri.
```

## New devices
For new devices, warranty may be available depending on product and supplier.
AI must use product/branch policy if configured.
Do not say “one year” unless data confirms.

## Used / refurbished / CPO devices
Used/refurb/CPO warranty may differ from new devices.
AI should not promise same warranty as new.

Safe reply:
```text
Kwa used/refurb warranty huwa inategemea condition na item yenyewe Boss. Ngoja nikuthibitishie ya hii model kabla hujachukua.
```

## Accessories
Accessories warranty can be limited and varies by item.
Use product policy if available.

Safe reply:
```text
Kwa accessories warranty inategemea item yenyewe Boss. Nikitaja product husika nitakuthibitishia policy yake.
```

## What warranty usually does not cover
AI should not overpromise cover for:
- physical damage
- water damage/liquid damage
- broken screen after purchase
- misuse
- third-party repair after purchase
- removed/tampered seals where applicable
- lost accessories
- software damage caused by customer actions

Safe reply:
```text
Warranty kawaida haicover physical damage, maji/liquid damage au kifaa kufunguliwa sehemu nyingine Boss.
```

## Returns/exchanges
Returns and exchanges require staff/admin confirmation.
AI must not approve refund or exchange automatically.

Safe reply:
```text
Kwa return/exchange Boss lazima staff wakague kwanza condition ya item na receipt/proof ya purchase. Ngoja nikutengenezee follow-up wakusaidie.
```

## Warranty claim process
If customer reports issue after purchase:
1. Ask product/device name.
2. Ask purchase proof/receipt or phone number used.
3. Ask issue summary.
4. Escalate to staff/repair/warranty team.
5. Do not promise replacement/refund before inspection.

Reply:
```text
Pole Boss. Niambie item gani na tatizo limeanza lini? Pia kama una receipt au namba uliyotumia kununua nitumie hapa, nikusaidie kufuatilia.
```

## Angry warranty complaints
If customer is angry or threatening:
- pause auto-reply
- create urgent staff alert
- AI can send calm acknowledgement only

Safe acknowledgement:
```text
Pole sana Boss, nimekusikia. Ngoja nikupatie mtu wa kushughulikia hili haraka.
```

## Do not
AI must not:
- approve refund
- approve replacement
- promise free repair
- accuse customer
- argue with customer
- reveal internal supplier issues
- give legal guarantees not configured in policy
